# 💪 Muscle-Meister: Your Personal Trainer

Transform your fitness journey with **Muscle-Meister**—a dynamic app designed to guide, motivate, and track your workouts, diet, and progress. Whether you're bulking up, slimming down, or getting shredded, we’ve got you covered!

[![Deploy on Netlify](https://img.shields.io/badge/Deploy%20on-Netlify-00C7B7?style=for-the-badge&logo=netlify&logoColor=white)](https://muscle-meister.netlify.app/)

---

## 🚀 Features

### 📅 **Smart Workout Plans**
- **Day-Based Routines**: Tailored workouts for each day (Mon-Sat) with rest days optimized for your physique goals.
- **Body Part Focus**: 20+ handpicked exercises for **chest, legs, arms, core, and back**.
- **Cardio & Warm-Up**: Detailed cardio sessions and warm-up routines to prep your muscles.

### 🥗 **Custom Diet Plans**
- **Goal-Oriented Nutrition**: 
  - 🏋️ **Bulk Up**: High-protein, calorie-surplus meals.
  - 🏃 **Slim Down**: Balanced macros with calorie deficit.
  - 🔥 **Get Shredded**: Lean meals with timed carb cycling.

### 🎮 **In-App Gaming**
- Beat workout boredom with quick, fun games to keep your motivation high!

### 🏆 **Hall of Fame**
- Share your success story, progress photos, and savings (vs. traditional PT costs) to get featured! Contact us via the app.

## 🛠 Tech Stack
- **Frontend**: React + TypeScript, Framer Motion (animations)
- **Styling**: CSS Modules, React Fonts
- **Backend**: Node.js (future integration)
- **Deployment**: Netlify
